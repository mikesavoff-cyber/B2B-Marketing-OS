

AI Prompt

# Customer Persona

**Prompt Template**  
---

Build me a persona of a **\[job title\]** at **\[industry/company \+ size/geography\]** with **\[roles/skills/responsibility\]**. This person is seeking help with **\[challenge/problem/task\]** and is considering **\[product/service\]**.

List their hopes/dreams, fears/concerns, emotional triggers and decision criteria for **\[hiring/contracting\]** a **\[partner/vendor/provider\]**. 

**Example**  
---

Build me a persona of **an office manager**  at **a midsized professional services firm in Chicago**  who **is responsible for ordering office supplies, including coffee**. 

This person is **looking for a new coffee delivery company and is concerned with taste, consistency and team morale.**

List their hopes/dreams, fears/concerns, emotional triggers and decision criteria for **contracting** a **vendor**.
