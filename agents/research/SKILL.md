# Research Skill

## Purpose

Turn a PMM question into an evidence-backed research brief by identifying what must be learned, gathering relevant evidence, synthesizing patterns, and exposing uncertainty and gaps.

## Trigger when

- a PMM project requires market, customer, category, competitor, or product research
- a downstream strategic decision lacks sufficient evidence
- existing evidence needs synthesis across heterogeneous sources

## Do not trigger when

- the task is straightforward copy production from approved inputs
- the required facts are already established and no research is needed
- research would only create volume without changing a decision

## Required inputs

- research question or decision to inform
- project / company context
- known evidence and constraints

## Procedure

1. Translate the request into explicit research questions.
2. Identify the decision each question should inform.
3. Separate known facts from assumptions and open questions.
4. Determine the highest-value evidence sources.
5. Retrieve relevant internal knowledge and external/live evidence as available.
6. Evaluate source relevance and reliability.
7. Synthesize patterns without presenting correlation or repetition as proof.
8. Identify contradictions and competing explanations.
9. State what the evidence supports, what it does not support, and what remains unknown.
10. Recommend next research steps only when they could materially change a decision.

## Output

- research objective
- decision to inform
- questions
- evidence collected
- key findings
- contradictions
- implications
- confidence
- assumptions
- evidence gaps
- recommended next steps
