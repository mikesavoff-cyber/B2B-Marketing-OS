## Interview Guide

Story-framed, mapped to the five rings. The same guide is used for everyone. Not every question is
asked every time.

```markdown
**Intro**: why we're doing persona research. Recording consent.

**Background**
- Tell me about your role and what you're responsible for this year.

**Priority initiatives** (absent of our product)
- Walk me through what was happening when you first started looking into [problem area].
- Why did that become a priority then? (Ask why again.)

**Success factors**
- If this went perfectly, what would be different for the team? For you personally?

**Perceived barriers**
- What almost stopped you, or would stop you, from buying something like this?

**Buyer's journey**
- Walk me through each step from first looking to deciding. Who was involved at each step?
- What information did you need, and where did you find it? (Name specific sources.)

**Decision criteria**
- What did you compare options on? Why those criteria?
- Whose recommendation mattered most?

**Close**
- What would you replace it with if it disappeared tomorrow?
```
