# Stage 5: Test and roll out

Goal: find out whether the positioning lands with real buyers before anything is rebuilt
around it. Done means tested and approved. A finished draft is not done.

## Read first

1. Dunford §6: the eight-component sales pitch. §7: the pitch-to-positioning map.
2. Dunford §9 principle: "If the sales team can't use the positioning in a real conversation,
   the positioning is wrong."
3. Dell L8 (`...Test Your Positioning Story and Roll It Out...md`): pilot then full rollout,
   deck vs. landing page order, testing rules, bottom-of-funnel-first rollout.
4. Fletch §17: the homepage test.
5. MKT1 guide, process section: share the statement company-wide, link out to the research.

## Do

1. **Pitch storyboard.** Build Dunford's eight components from the canvas and story. This is
   the first test: can the story be told in order without gaps?
2. **Test plan.**
   - Sales deck: simple promise first, then change, stakes, promised land, villain,
     superpowers, proof (Dell L8's Zuora order).
   - Landing page: simple promise on top, proof higher and repeated (Dell L8).
   - At least five best-customer participants per test (Dell L8). Screen for the best-customer
     profile. Ask "why" and "how." No leading questions.
   - Say what result would count as a fail before running.
3. **Results log.** Mike or the team runs the tests. This skill never invents results. Log
   quotes, where people leaned in, and where they got confused.
4. **Revise.** Send each failure back to the stage that caused it: confusion about what it is
   goes to Stage 3's "what." Wrong comparison goes to Stage 2. Flat reaction goes to Stage 4.
5. **Rollout plan.** Bottom of funnel first (intent search, retargeting, landing pages), then
   middle, then top (Dell L8). Internal rollout: the statement goes to the whole company, with
   links to the research (MKT1).

Category creators: Dell L8 notes early tests may reject a new category. If Mike chose Fletch
strategy 3 in Stage 2, record a negative test as a risk. It is not an automatic kill.

## Checkpoint with Mike

Approval of the constitution. Until tests run, its status line says "hypothesis." After Mike
approves test results, bump the version and set the status to "approved."

## Output

The test plan, fail criteria, and results log go in the working notes. The constitution gets the
winning promise, any statement changes the tests forced, and an updated status line.
