# Red team

*Added 2026-09-28 at Mike's request.* Before Mike sees the statements, attack them. The red team
is not a competitor analysis and not a list of everything the product beats. It is three
adversaries trying to break the draft's load-bearing claims.

## What gets attacked

Only the claims that hold the positioning up:

1. The audience (for / not for).
2. The category frame.
3. The current ways chosen to fight.
4. The one differentiator.
5. The proof behind each statement.
6. The recommended simple promise.

## The three adversaries

| Adversary | Their question | Ammunition |
|---|---|---|
| **The strongest competitor's rep** | "We do that too. Why them?" | Each competitor's own positioning, reverse-engineered from `competitor-research` into a who / what / why-better statement. Run the name-swap test against every one. |
| **The best-fit buyer** | "Is that how I'd describe my problem? Why switch from what I do today? Prove it." | The ledger's customer language and objection rows; buyer personas if they exist |
| **The status quo** | "Do nothing." "Build it in-house." "Keep the spreadsheet." | The current-ways table; any insider evidence of customers building their own |

Also attack the evidence itself: list every claim that rests on one anecdote, an insider opinion,
a roadmap promise, or low-confidence rows.

## Output (working notes)

| Claim | Adversary | Attack | Evidence for the attack | Verdict | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Verdict:** holds / weakened / breaks.
- **Breaks:** rewrite the claim, or send it back to the stage that produced it.
- **Weakened:** keep it, and list it as an accepted weakness.
- **Holds:** no change. Say why the attack failed.

## What to expect

- Two to five attacks with teeth. A red team that finds nothing wasn't hard enough. One that
  rewrites everything means Stage 2 was wrong, so reopen it instead of patching statements.
- Change only where the attack's evidence is stronger than the claim's. Don't change a claim to
  look more sophisticated.

## What reaches the constitution

Nothing about the process. Only:
- The changed statements, in place.
- Two or three accepted weaknesses, under open questions, each with the evidence that would
  resolve it.

The chat summary at checkpoint 2 lists the attacks that changed something.
