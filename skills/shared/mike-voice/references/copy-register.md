# Copy register

For website, landing pages, value props, messaging, taglines, ads, emails, in-app and launch
copy. Exemplars: `copy/atomus.md` and the top of `posthog/messaging ideas.md`.

## Become the customer

Before the first line, write as the customer, not as a marketer who studied them. Mike calls
this "extreme empathy": "embodying the icp and persona to dig as deep as possible and identify
the biggest emotional drivers" (`posthog interview.md`). His four questions:

- What keeps you up at night and distracts you all day?
- What would you be willing to do to get the best case done?
- Why is this such a big pain in your ass?
- What do you actually want to achieve, and why?

Research produces "designers struggle with consistency across projects." The customer's
voice produces "you're digging through thousands of components for the ones you need the
most." Write the second.

## Inputs

Copy carries approved messaging. If a `messaging` or ICP artifact exists for the project, the
claims come from it. This skill supplies the words. With no approved messaging, the draft is
**[Hypothesis]** copy, and it says so.

## Moves

- **Stack the problem before the product.** When the product appears, it should feel like relief, not a pitch.
- **Scenes, not labels.** "UX that makes customers scream at their phones," not "poor UX."
- **Escalate with a question:** "Wanna hear the really bad part?"
- **"But" raises the stakes:** "Today, being a good designer is easy. But it's not enough."
- **Parenthetical truth:** "(while being rushed and uninspired by clients and PMs)"
- **Outcome as a scene the reader wants:** "Make your boss say 'You're too good for us.'" · "Get ahead of developers and make them like you."
- **Blunt fear, in the customer's words:** "don't let ai bankrupt your business" · "identify and predict when shit will hit the fan"
- **Insider wordplay** for the audience: "a pain in the Behance" works for designers, and only for designers.
- **One lead benefit.** The rest support it. No three equal value props.
- **Specificity is the empathy.** Name the customer's actual day: "being rushed and uninspired by clients and PMs."

## High-stakes buyers

In finance, security, or enterprise buying, trust beats cleverness. "low-commitment language
like 'try automated portfolios today' might actually undermine trust" (`Revolut/Revolut.md`).
Lead with why to trust you, not with the mechanics. Save the jokes for the practitioner, not
the approver.
