# MKT1 - Positioning statement template
Source: MKT1 paid template library (Emily Kramer), companion to `The MKT1 Guide to positioning.md`. Converted from Mike's copy of the Google Doc/.docx.
Covers: the one-page format for sharing a final positioning statement with the whole company, plus the "summary of decisions" behind it. Images are transcribed as text, marked [Figure].

## HOW TO USE

For context on how to use this template, please read MKT1's [positioning guide newsletter](https://newsletter.mkt1.co/p/positioning-guide).

This template is intended for your final positioning statement to share with your entire company, you can choose what to include in the summary of decisions section below and what links you provide to additional research. But always include the positioning statement at the top.

**[Figure: How to write a positioning statement.]**

Product type & comparator → (drives positioning) → Who is it for? → What is it? → Why is it better? → Positioning statement → (refine for ICPs & segments) → Messaging. The three middle boxes are the "3 parts of positioning statement."

# SUMMARY OF DECISIONS

### PRODUCT TYPE & COMPARATOR

**We selected from these 4 options:**

1. **10x better** than incumbent(s) in existing category
2. **A new way** to solve a problem currently solved with multiple workflows, tools, services, and processes
3. **A vertical solution** for a niche audience, instead of using a horizontal solution
4. **A product you buy** vs build, that replaces building and managing a solution internally

**[Figure: How to choose your product type.]**

| | 10x better (in existing category) | New way (aka new category) | Vertical solution | Buy vs build |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Definition | Better solution to known problem in existing category | No established solution to problem | Solution for a niche audience | External, productized solution |
| Comparator to position against | Current product leaders in category | Combo of multiple workflows (tools, services, processes) | Horizontal solution not tailored to audience | Building & managing internal process or tool |
| Awareness level | Problem aware: yes. Solution aware: yes | Problem aware: rarely. Solution aware: no | Problem aware: usually. Solution aware: no | Problem aware: usually. Solution aware: rarely |
| Examples | Mercury was a 10x better way to bank | Slack was a new way to communicate & work | Toast was built to manage restaurants | Buy Stripe; don't build your own payments flow |

*Note: this version defines 10x better as "better solution to known problem in existing category." The newsletter figure says "better solution in existing category."*

### WHAT IS IT?

- We determined our audience is **problem aware but not solution aware.** Given this, we made our "what is it" focused on explaining the solution very clearly.
- **Our product is a ______________________.**
- Here's how we determine the level of awareness of our primary audience:
  - Problem aware = realizes there's a problem or need for a solution
  - Solution aware = realizes solutions exist for their problem
  - Product aware = aware of our specific product already (usually only applies to established, late stage companies)

**[Figure: Awareness levels.]**

| Awareness level | Definition | How to write "what it is" |
|---|---|---|
| Problem aware | Recognizes a problem or need, doesn't know solutions exist | Remind of problem & explain solution |
| Solution aware | Knows solutions to problem exist | Describe your specific solution |
| Product aware | Knows your company exists as a solution to problem | Explain your product's unique approach to solution |
