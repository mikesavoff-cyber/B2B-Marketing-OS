# B2B Marketing OS

This repository contains:
- source knowledge
- industry expertise and methodology about:
- product marketing (positioning, messaging, storytelling, pricing & packaging, product launches, segmentation, ICP and persona definition, and frameworks by leading PMM voices like Fletch PMM, April Dunford, MKT1, and CXL)
- growth strategy (growth models, growth levers, growth channels, product-market fit, retention & engagement loops, growth process, north star metric, and understanding growth)
- content strategy (search ranking factors for ai, websites, conversion, and visitor psychology, demand generation, measuring)
- GTM (creative development, data collection for gtm, lead gen, landing page optimization, mvs framework) 
- reusable marketing skills
- executable workflows
- project outputs
- evaluations

Knowledge is organized by discipline.

Use skills for reusable capabilities.
Use workflows for multi-stage marketing jobs.

Prefer relevant, task-specific context over loading the entire knowledge base.

combining them.
